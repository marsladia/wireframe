Behind the innovation

AlwayStart wasn't built to be a feature inside someone else's catalog. It was built to retire the dead battery for good.

The origin signal

A deal that didn't happen...for the right reason.

In 2025, the AlwayStart founders, after massive industry research, were at a crossroads. They could either partner with a major battery-charger company but lose control of the quality and function of their innovative product or bootstrap it to market themselves.

They chose the road less traveled and launched their incredible product themselves. The result: proactive, automatic battery protection that helps prevents battery failure instead of reacting to it. The Kickstarter, "No More Dead Batteries," raised over $250,000 from hundreds of backers, many times its goal.

As Indiana Jones might say, "They chose wisely!" The rest of the story might soon be told in automotive history. Stay tuned!
